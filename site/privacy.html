Privacy & Local Data

Short version: your words stay in your browser. There are no accounts, no logins, and no server that receives what you type. The longer version — what exactly stays, and the few things that do leave — is below.

What stays on your device

  • Everything you type. Brain dumps, session plans, scores, timers, and comfort settings (text size, font, motion, read-aloud state) live in localStorage in your own browser.
  • Nothing syncs. Clearing site data in your browser wipes it; nobody else can see it because it never leaves.
  • No accounts, no health records anywhere else. There is nowhere for your data to go — the site has no backend.
Principle
A tool for vulnerable moments should not phone home with them.
If you want something gone, clear it in the tool (or clear site data) and it is gone everywhere, because everywhere is one place: your browser.

What leaves your device

  • Page-visit counts. Most pages include a lightweight analytics snippet that records that a page was visited — not what you typed, clicked, or saved. It measures footfall, never content.
  • Voice dictation audio. The mic button uses your browser's built-in speech recognition, which sends audio to your browser vendor's speech service (Google, Apple, or Microsoft, depending on your browser) for transcription. If that bothers you, use the keyboard instead — it works everywhere here.
  • Font downloads. Pages fetch fonts from Google Fonts, and the dyslexia-friendly font from its CDN the first time you enable it. Those requests tell the CDN an IP address loaded a font — standard web behavior, no content attached.

What this site is not

A gentle note
None of this is medical advice or a diagnosis, and no tool here replaces a therapist, doctor, or your own judgement. These are scaffolding — external structure for a brain that does better with structure outside its own head.
